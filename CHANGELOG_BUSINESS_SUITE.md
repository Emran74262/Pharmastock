# PharmaStock Business Suite Update

This update adds the requested professional pharmacy-management features while preserving the existing PharmaStock functions.

## Added
- Dashboard analytics: today's sales/profit, monthly sales/profit, stock value, 30-day sales chart, best sellers.
- Notifications: low stock, out-of-stock, expiry/expired alerts and recent stock-in notifications.
- Profit & Loss reporting with sales, cost and gross-profit calculations using batch purchase prices.
- Professional invoices with pharmacy profile (name, phone, address, logo), payment method/status, paid amount and Print / Save PDF.
- Advanced medicine search by name, generic, company/category, manufacturer, barcode and batch.
- Barcode support with USB scanner/manual barcode entry and camera scanning where supported by the browser.
- Supplier database, contact details, purchase history and supplier outstanding amounts.
- Customer database, contact details, purchase history and customer outstanding amounts.
- Payment methods: Cash, bKash, Nagad, Card, Bank and Mixed; plus Paid/Partial/Due status.
- Stronger user management: enable/disable accounts and admin staff-activity audit history.
- Admin-only protection for sensitive delete/permanent-delete operations.
- Backup now includes suppliers, customers and deleted-invoice backups.
